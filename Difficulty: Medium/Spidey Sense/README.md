<h2><a href="https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/problems/spidey-sense5556/1">Spidey Sense</a></h2><h3>Difficulty Level : Difficulty: Medium</h3><hr><div class="problems_problem_content__Xm_eO" style="--text-color: var(--problem-text-color);"><p data-start="937" data-end="1049"><span style="font-size: 14pt;">Given an <strong>n × m</strong> grid <strong>mat[][]</strong> consisting of the characters 'O', 'B', and 'W', where 'O' represents an open cell, 'B' represents a bomb, and 'W' represents a wall. A move can be made from a cell to any of its four adjacent cells (up, down, left, and right), and walls cannot be crossed.</span></p><p data-start="937" data-end="1049"><span style="font-size: 14pt;">Replace each cell in the grid as follows:</span></p><ul><li data-start="937" data-end="1049"><span style="font-size: 14pt;">Replace every 'B' with 0.</span></li><li data-start="937" data-end="1049"><span style="font-size: 14pt;">Replace every 'W' with -1.</span></li><li data-start="937" data-end="1049"><span style="font-size: 14pt;">Replace every 'O' with the minimum number of moves required to reach the nearest bomb without passing through any wall. If an open cell cannot reach any bomb, replace it with -1.</span></li></ul><p data-start="937" data-end="1049"><span style="font-size: 14pt;">Return the resulting integer matrix.</span></p><p><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Examples :</strong></span></p><pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input: </strong>mat[][] = {</span><span style="font-size: 18px;">{O, O, O}, {W, B, B}, {W, O, O</span><span style="font-size: 18px;">}}</span>
<span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Output:</strong> {</span><span style="font-size: 18px;">{2, 1, 1}, {-1, 0, 0}, {-1, 1, 1</span><span style="font-size: 18px;">}}</span>
<span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Explanation: <br><img src="https://media.geeksforgeeks.org/img-practice/prod/addEditProblem/930986/Web/Other/blobid0_1784806810.webp" width="239" height="110"><br></strong></span><span style="font-size: 18px;">The wall cells (1,0) and (2,0) are replaced with -1, and the bomb cells (1,1) and (1,2) are replaced with 0. Each open cell is assigned the minimum distance to the nearest bomb without crossing any wall. Thus, the open cells (0,0), (0,1), (0,2), (2,1), and (2,2) are replaced with 2, 1, 1, 1, and 1 respectively.
</span></pre><pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input: </strong></span><span style="font-size: 18px;">mat[][]</span><span style="font-size: 18px;"> = {</span><span style="font-size: 18px;">{O, O}, </span></span><span style="font-size: 18px;">{O, O}</span><span style="font-size: 18px;">}</span> 
<span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Output:</strong> {{-1, -1}, {-1, -1}}<br><strong>Explanation:<br><img src="https://media.geeksforgeeks.org/img-practice/prod/addEditProblem/930986/Web/Other/blobid1_1784806879.webp" height="100"></strong>
There is no bomb in the grid, so none of the open cells can reach a bomb. Hence, all open cells are replaced with -1.
